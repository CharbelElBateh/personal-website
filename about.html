I'm Charbel Al Bateh — an AI engineer and researcher, and a Computer Engineering student at the Lebanese American University, working across machine learning, LLM reasoning and quantum machine learning.

Most of my time goes into two kinds of craft: shipping systems that hold up under real traffic, and writing up the small, reusable ideas that made them possible. I like systems that are small, legible, and honest about what they do not know.

Before INMIND.AI I built 3D point-cloud pipelines and synthetic-data augmentation in BMW Group's R&D, and shipped software at Invigo, MERAKI and Galactech.io. I assist in the microelectronics and digital-testing labs at LAU, and chair the IEEE Student Branch.
