## Direction contract

THESIS: A research portfolio staged like a studio reel: one lit, physical 3D cluster that the visitor pushes, scrolls through and breaks open. Refuses the academic-homepage default (text column, headshot, bullet list); the classical world gives it its own voice instead of a generic studio look.

OWN-WORLD: True black page, cream text (#e8e2d1), one gold (#c9a96e). Cormorant Garamond display with gold italic emphasis, Inter text, JetBrains Mono gold labels. Sharp fine-line frames with gold corner brackets, pill buttons, gold "+" registration marks, Greek ordinals, meander bands. Objects: classical fragments (fluted drums, capitals, Greek-key tiles, tesserae, orbs, laurel rings) in veined marble, gold, obsidian and night-stone, inside a faint gold star field.

STORY: Visitor meets the name and one-line role, plays with the cluster, scrolls: the frame swells to full bleed, the camera falls into the cluster, the statement takes the screen, then selected work and the way to contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (wordmark left; "Get in touch" ink pill and "Menu" pill right; under 560px only Menu, which carries Contact, for width). Role statement at ~3.6vw across the top two-thirds width. Below it a dark rounded stage filling the rest of the viewport, cluster centred, giant white name set into the stage's lower-left. Hairline with + marks and "Scroll to explore" under the stage.

FORM: Pinned scroll journey with physics stage. Concept roll waived by the user's explicit pin: "I WANT SOMETHING GRANDIOSE ... I WANT THE SAME VIBE" (reference lusion.co). No seed key.
